DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Observe. Understand. Balance. Adapt.

01

Observe before acting

Collect device context and establish a baseline before considering adaptation.

02

Prefer evidence to assumptions

Confidence, semantic meaning and measured outcomes guide whether a candidate is justified.

03

Keep authority explicit

Capability discovery does not grant permission. Policy, safety gates and bounded actuators remain authoritative.

04

Recover when evidence degrades

Verification, mutation journaling, safety holds and rollback are part of the adaptation path.
